# NDA — disclosing party (example)

Example shipped with legalsuite to show the playbook format. It is not approved
policy and not legal advice. Use your own approved playbooks for real matters.

## Scope
NDAs before a commercial deal, investment or cooperation where our side
discloses business, technical or financial information. Not for employment
confidentiality, M&A clean-team arrangements or public-sector counterparties
(Registr smluv rules apply there).

## Positions

| ID | Clause / issue | Req | Preferred (GREEN) | Fallback (YELLOW) | Walk-away (RED) | Approver | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Definition of Confidential Information | must | All information disclosed in any form, marked or not, including the fact and content of the negotiations | Information marked or identified as confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential; oral disclosures included if identified at the time | Only information marked "confidential" in writing | senior associate | Marking discipline fails in practice; oral and visual disclosures are where leaks start |
| P2 | Exceptions | must | Four standard exceptions (public without breach, lawfully known before, independently developed, received from a third party without restriction), each to be proven by the Recipient with written records | Same exceptions, burden of proof not stated | Any "generally known in the industry" or "residual knowledge" exception | partner | A residuals clause turns the NDA into a licence |
| P3 | Compelled disclosure | must | Prompt prior notice where lawful, disclosure limited to what is required, cooperation on protective measures | Notice "where practicable" | No notice obligation | senior associate | Lets us seek protection before disclosure |
| P4 | Term | must | Obligations survive 5 years after the end of the NDA; trade secrets protected as long as they remain trade secrets | 3 years; trade secrets carved out without time limit | Under 2 years, or no trade-secret carve-out | partner | Protection must outlast the value of the information |
| P5 | Permitted recipients | must | Employees and professional advisers with a need to know, bound by equivalent duties; Recipient liable for them as for itself | Affiliates added on the same terms | Recipient not liable for its representatives | senior associate | Liability must follow the information |
| P6 | Contractual penalty (*smluvní pokuta*) | should | Penalty per breach in an amount reflecting the value of the information, payable in addition to full damages | Penalty only, credited against damages | Neither a penalty nor uncapped damages | partner | Damages from a leak are hard to prove; the penalty makes the NDA enforceable in practice |
| P7 | Limitation of liability | must | No cap for breach of confidentiality | Cap at a multiple of the penalty, excluding intent and gross negligence | Cap below the penalty, or exclusion of damages for breach of confidentiality | partner | A low cap prices the leak for the Recipient |
| P8 | Return and destruction | should | On request within 10 business days, with written confirmation; backup copies remain confidential | On request, without a time limit | No return or destruction duty | senior associate | Keeps control of the information after the talks end |
| P9 | Governing law and forum | must | Czech law; Czech courts at our seat | Arbitration (Arbitration Court attached to the Czech Chamber of Commerce and the Agricultural Chamber), seat in Prague | Foreign law and foreign courts with no recognition route in the CZ/EU | partner | Interim relief and enforcement where the information is |
| P10 | No licence, no warranty, no obligation to proceed | should | Express in all three | Licence exclusion only | Any licence or feedback right granted to the Recipient | senior associate | Disclosure must not be read as a grant |

## Clause library

### P4 — Term
Preferred wording:
> The obligations under this Agreement continue for five (5) years after this Agreement terminates. Obligations relating to information that constitutes a trade secret continue for as long as that information remains a trade secret.

Fallback wording:
> The obligations under this Agreement continue for three (3) years after this Agreement terminates. Obligations relating to information that constitutes a trade secret continue for as long as that information remains a trade secret.

### P6 — Contractual penalty
Preferred wording:
> For each breach of its obligations under this Agreement, the Recipient shall pay the Discloser a contractual penalty of [● amount]. Payment of the contractual penalty does not affect the Discloser's right to compensation for damage in full, including damage exceeding the contractual penalty.

Fallback wording:
> For each breach of its obligations under this Agreement, the Recipient shall pay the Discloser a contractual penalty of [● amount]. The contractual penalty is credited against compensation for damage arising from the same breach.

## Standard definitions
- "Confidential Information": per P1, preferred wording.
- "Representatives": employees, officers and professional advisers who need
  the information for the Purpose.

## Red flags
- A residuals or "general skills and knowledge" clause.
- A feedback, improvement or licence grant to the Recipient.
- A non-compete or exclusivity hidden in an NDA.
- A one-way NDA running the wrong way (we are the Recipient).
- A standstill or non-solicitation we did not ask for.

## Negotiation sequence
1. Hold P2 (no residuals) and P7 (no low cap). These are not traded.
2. Trade P4 (5 → 3 years) for P6 (keep the penalty).
3. Concede P8 timing and P10 warranty wording last.

## Jurisdiction notes
- CZ: trade secret protection, pre-contractual confidentiality, the
  contractual penalty and its relation to damages, judicial moderation of
  excessive penalties, and the limits on excluding liability all come from the
  Civil Code. Resolve each in the citation ledger before relying on it (see the
  `cz` jurisdiction pack §4 and §15).
