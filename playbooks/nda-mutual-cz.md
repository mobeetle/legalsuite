# NDA — mutual

Seed playbook shipped with legalsuite. It ships as draft
(not approved policy) and is not legal advice. The positions are a balanced
commercial stance for a mutual NDA; confirm them against your own approved
playbook and the current law before any use.

## Scope
Mutual non-disclosure agreements before a commercial deal, diligence or
cooperation where each party discloses and receives information. Not for one-way
NDAs (see the recipient playbook), employment confidentiality, M&A clean-team
arrangements or public-sector counterparties (Registr smluv rules apply there).

| P1 | Definition of Confidential Information | must | Information disclosed by either party in any form, marked in writing, or, if disclosed orally or visually, identified as confidential at the time and confirmed in writing within 10 business days | Information a reasonable person would understand to be confidential; oral disclosures identified as confidential at the time | An asymmetric definition that protects only one party's information, or an unmarked "everything disclosed" definition | senior associate | A mutual NDA must define both parties' information on the same terms |

## Clause library

### P2 — Exceptions and carve-outs
Preferred wording:
> Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement by the receiving party; (b) was lawfully in the receiving party's possession before disclosure by the disclosing party; (c) is independently developed by the receiving party without use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence. Each exception applies equally to both parties.

Fallback wording:
> Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement by the receiving party; (b) was lawfully in the receiving party's possession before disclosure by the disclosing party; (c) is independently developed by the receiving party without use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence. A narrow mutual residuals clause applies to information retained in the unaided memory of individuals, but not to trade secrets.

### P3 — Term
Preferred wording:
> The obligations under this Agreement continue for three (3) years after this Agreement terminates, on the same terms for each party. Obligations relating to information that constitutes a trade secret (*obchodní tajemství*) continue for as long as that information remains a trade secret.

Fallback wording:
> The obligations under this Agreement continue for five (5) years after this Agreement terminates, on the same terms for each party. Obligations relating to information that constitutes a trade secret (*obchodní tajemství*) continue for as long as that information remains a trade secret.

### P7 — Contractual penalty
Preferred wording:
> This Agreement creates no contractual penalty (*smluvní pokuta*). The parties' remedies are compensation for damage and injunctive relief, in each case subject to the limitation of liability in this Agreement.

Fallback wording:
> For each breach of its obligations under this Agreement, the breaching party shall pay the other party a contractual penalty of [● amount], applied symmetrically. The contractual penalty is credited against any compensation for damage arising from the same breach.

### P8 — Limitation of liability
Preferred wording:
> Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to [● amount], equally for both parties. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost profit or loss of opportunity. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for damage caused intentionally or by gross negligence.

Fallback wording:
> Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the aggregate amount of any contractual penalty payable plus [● amount], applied equally for both parties. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for damage caused intentionally or by gross negligence.

## Standard definitions
- "Confidential Information": per P1, preferred wording, covering both parties'
  information.
- "Purpose": the mutual evaluation of the potential transaction or cooperation
  between the parties.
- "Disclosing party" and "receiving party": each party in the role it holds for
  a given disclosure.

## Red flags
- Asymmetry: obligations, carve-outs or remedies that bind only one party.
- A residuals or "general skills and knowledge" clause reaching trade secrets.
- A feedback, improvement or licence grant to one party.
- A non-compete, exclusivity or one-way non-solicit hidden in the NDA.
- A penalty owed by one party only, or payable on top of uncapped damages.
- A perpetual term for information that is not a trade secret.
- Foreign law and foreign forum with no recognition route in the CZ/EU.

## Negotiation sequence
1. Hold symmetry: P2 (equal carve-outs) and P8 (equal cap). An asymmetric
   mutual NDA is a one-way NDA in disguise, so these are not traded.
2. Trade P3 (three → five years) for P7 (drop the penalty, or keep it
   reciprocal and credited against damages).
3. Concede P9 timing and P4 purpose wording last.

## Jurisdiction notes
- CZ: the Civil Code (zákon č. 89/2012 Sb.) governs pre-contractual
  confidentiality, trade secret (*obchodní tajemství*), the contractual penalty
  (*smluvní pokuta*) under §§ 2048–2051 and its judicial moderation
  (§ 2051 ⚠ verify), and the limits on excluding or limiting liability
  (§ 2898). Where a penalty or a cap is agreed, it must operate symmetrically.
  Resolve each in the citation ledger before relying on it (see the `cz`
  jurisdiction pack §4 and §15).
- CZ: an NDA needs no special form, but any penalty and any restraint must be
  certain and limited in time to be enforceable.
