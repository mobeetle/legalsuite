# Hearing and witness preparation

Everything here derives from the claims matrix and the fact map. Prepare
nothing that isn't tied to an element we must prove or an element they must
prove.

## Case theory (one paragraph)

The story that explains all the critical documents and the undisputed facts,
favours our side on every disputed element, and can be told in two minutes.
Each sentence maps to matrix elements. If a critical document doesn't fit the
theory, the theory is wrong. Fix the theory, not the document.

## Hearing plan (`hearing-plan.md`)

```
| # | Element (I-id) | What we must show / rebut | Through (witness, exhibit, expert) | Their likely answer | Our response |
```

Add the procedural points: what can still be pleaded or offered at this
hearing (preclusion, pack §8 (Civil procedure & relief requirements)), applications to make (evidence, adjournment,
interim relief), and the costs statement if the forum expects one (pack §9 (Costs & fees)).

## Exhibit list (`exhibits.md`)

```
| Exhibit | X-id | Description | Date | Source / custody | Authenticity | Supports (F-ids) | Offered in (submission §) |
```

Every exhibit offered appears in a submission. Every exhibit whose authenticity
is `disputed` or `unknown` has a plan: the original, a witness of origin,
metadata, an expert.

## Witness outlines (one per witness, a view of the fact map)

```
# Witness outline — <role/initials>
Relation to the parties: …   Interest in the outcome: …
Topics (I-ids): …
Facts within their knowledge: F… (with the source of their knowledge: saw / heard / was told)
Documents to put to them: X…
Risks: inconsistencies with documents or other witnesses; things they don't know
```

- Questions are **open and neutral** for our own witnesses. Never script
  answers, and never put facts to a witness that they don't know first-hand.
- A written statement, where the forum uses them, is in the witness's own
  words and reviewed by them. The skill may organise topics; it doesn't write
  testimony.

## Cross-examination points

From the inconsistencies list and the fact map: for each opposing witness,
the facts where their account conflicts with a document (`X`) or with their
own earlier statement. Each point is one short, closed question with the
exhibit that proves it. Take it no further than the document goes.

## Anticipated objections
