# AML (CZ) — regime pack

How to use: this pack is a seed obligation register, not the law itself. Use §2
to decide applicability and §3 to fix the client's role, then build the
obligation register from §4. Every obligation used in a deliverable is resolved
to the current text (version in force on the EPOCH date) in the citation
ledger; never cite this pack as authority. ⚠ marks items not verified against an
official source at `reviewed_at`. If today is past `review_by`, warn the user
that the pack may be stale before relying on it.

## 2. Scope tests

1. **Is the client a *povinná osoba*?** (§ 2(1)) — credit/financial institutions
   incl. CASPs (a, b); real-estate buyers/sellers/brokers (rent brokerage only
   ≥ EUR 10,000/month) (d); auditors, tax advisors, accountants (e); notaries
   (custody) and **advocates** (g — see test 2); trust and company service
   providers (registered seat, nominee, formation) (h); art traders ≥ EUR 10,000
   (i); precious metals/stones ≥ EUR 10,000 (n); any trader accepting cash
   ≥ EUR 10,000 (§ 2(2)). Not carried on as business → out (§ 2(3)).
2. **Advocate in scope?** (§ 2(1)(g)) — only when holding client assets in custody
   (*úschova*) or acting for/on behalf of a client in: (1) purchase/sale of real
   estate or a business (*obchodní závod*) or its part; (2) managing money,
   securities or other assets, incl. opening/running accounts; (3) founding,
   managing or operating a corporation and raising funds for it; (4) setting up
   or administering a *svěřenský fond*/trust; (5) payments, transfers, deposits,
   withdrawals.
3. **Privilege carve-out** (§ 27(1)) — §§ 9, 15a, 18(1) and 24 do not apply to
   information obtained in providing legal advice, criminal defence, or
   representation in / advice on court proceedings (before, during, after).
4. **Identification trigger?** (§ 7): transaction > **EUR 1,000**; always for a
   suspicious transaction, new business relationship, listed sectors.
5. **CDD trigger?** (§ 9): business relationship (throughout); occasional
   transaction ≥ **EUR 15,000**; PEP; high-risk third country; remote
   identification (§ 11(7)); gambling EUR 2,000; transfers ≥ EUR 1,000.
6. **Sanctions exposure?** — any obliged person, and anyone holding sanctioned
   property (§ 10 z. 69/2006 Sb.); EU regulations apply to all EU persons.

## 3. Roles

- **Povinná osoba** (§ 2) — obliged person; sector determines supervisor and
  internal-rules level.
- **Úvěrová/finanční instituce** (§ 2(1)(a)–(b)) — full regime, written SVZ.
- **Advokát / notář** (§ 2(1)(g)) — limited regime for listed transactions;
  reports via chamber (§ 27); supervised by ČAK/NK (§ 35); no written SVZ (§ 21(2)).
- **Auditor, daňový poradce, exekutor** (§ 26) — report via their chamber.
- **Kontaktní osoba** (§ 22) — contact person for FAÚ; **designated statutory-body
  member** (§ 22a).
- **Klient**, **skutečný majitel** (§ 4(4) ZAML → § 2(c), § 4 ZESM: > 25 % shares /
  voting / profit, or decisive influence; senior management fallback § 5),
  **politicky exponovaná osoba** (§ 4(5); measures ≥ 12 months after office,
  § 54).
- **FAÚ** — FIU and main supervisor (§ 35(1)); also sanctions authority
  (§ 8d z. 69/2006 Sb.).

## 6. Enforcement and sanctions

- **Supervisors:** FAÚ (main, § 35(1)); ČNB (financial market); customs; ČOI;
  **ČAK** for advocates (FAÚ may inspect only if chamber does not act within 60
  days; privileged documents only with chamber/court consent, § 37); chambers
  hear offences of § 2(1)(e), (g) persons (§ 52).
- **Fines (ZAML):** identification/CDD up to **CZK 10 m** (§ 44); failure to
  report up to **CZK 5 m** (§ 46); prevention duties up to CZK 1 m / 5 m / 10 m
  (§ 48); confidentiality CZK 200,000 (1 m aggravated) (§ 43); discrepancy
  failures (§ 44a). Serious/repeated/systematic: up to **2× benefit or CZK 30 m**;
  financial institutions **CZK 130 m or 10 % of net annual turnover** for
  credit/financial institutions, whichever higher. Limitation 5 years (10
  absolute) (§ 51a); publication of decisions 5 years (§ 53).
- **ZESM:** offence up to CZK 500,000 (§ 55); private-law consequences —
  unenforceability (§ 52), no dividend (§ 53), no voting (§ 54).
- **Sanctions:** up to CZK 50 m / 10 % turnover / 2× benefit (§ 19 z. 69/2006
  Sb.); criminal liability — *porušení mezinárodní sankce* § 410 TZ (gross
  negligence for dual-use/military goods § 410a); money laundering §§ 216–217 TZ.
- **Disciplinary:** ČAK disciplinary liability of advocates in parallel.
- **From July 2027:** administrative sanctions framework set by AMLD6 and
  national law (expected ZEOS) ⚠ verify ceilings once Czech act is adopted.

## 7. Contract touchpoints

- **Engagement letters (advocates):** AML identification/CDD consent and
  cooperation clause; right to suspend/terminate (§ 15); no tipping-off
  promise; retention 10 years overrides deletion requests.
- **Escrow (*advokátní úschova*) agreements:** CDD on all parties and BOs; source
  of funds; release conditions compatible with § 20 holds; sanctions
  representation; ČAK escrow rules ⚠ verify current usnesení představenstva ČAK.
- **M&A/SPA:** BO registration warranties (ZESM § 52–54 consequences); sanctions
  and AML compliance warranties; KYC cooperation covenants.
- **Reliance/outsourcing of CDD** (§ 11 takeover of identification): written
  arrangement, record access, liability remains with obliged person.
- **Sanctions clauses:** EU/UN/CZ (1/2023) lists; termination right; no
  "best efforts" where EU law imposes strict prohibition; Russia "no Russia"
  clause (Reg. 833/2014 art. 12g) for exports ⚠ verify applicability.

## 8. Interaction with other regimes

- **GDPR (`gdpr`)** — CDD processing under art. 6(1)(c); art. 10 data (criminal
  records) only as law permits; 10-year AML retention prevails over general
  schedules; tipping-off ban restricts art. 15 access (art. 23 GDPR basis via
  ZAML § 38 ⚠ verify).
- **Whistleblowing (`whistleblowing-cz`)** — obliged persons must run an
  internal channel regardless of size, accept anonymous reports, no outsourcing
  (§ 21(6)(b) ZAML).
- **Professional secrecy** — advocates' *povinnost mlčenlivosti* (§ 21 zákona o
  advokacii) vs AML reporting: resolved by § 27 (chamber route, privilege
  carve-out).
- **DORA / financial regulation (`dora`)** — CASP and financial institutions also
  under ČNB prudential/ICT rules; MiCA licensing (§ 29ba ZAML).
- **AMLR transition (from 10 July 2027):** directly applicable CDD rules
  (occasional transaction threshold EUR 10,000; transfers EUR 1,000; cash ID
  EUR 3,000 — AMLR art. 19); EUR 10,000 cash payment limit (art. 80; CZ already
  has a CZK 270,000 cash limit under zákon č. 254/2004 Sb. ⚠ verify); BO ownership threshold
  **25 % or more** (art. 52(1)) vs current CZ "more than 25 %" (§ 4 ZESM), lower
  thresholds possible for high-risk entities (art. 52(2)); record retention **5
  years** (+ up to 5 on authority request) (art. 77(3)) vs current 10; lawyers remain obliged entities
  (art. 3(3)(b)) with privilege carve-out; group-wide policies; Czech law will
  need a new act (ZEOS draft) for supervision, FIU and sanctions.
