# Whistleblowing (CZ) — regime pack

How to use: this pack is a seed obligation register, not the law itself. Use §2
to decide applicability and §3 to fix the client's role, then build the
obligation register from §4. Every obligation used in a deliverable is resolved
to the current text (version in force on the EPOCH date) in the citation
ledger; never cite this pack as authority. ⚠ marks items not verified against an
official source at `reviewed_at`. If today is past `review_by`, warn the user
that the pack may be stale before relying on it.

## 1. Instruments and status

ADOPTED and applicable:
- **Directive (EU) 2019/1937** (Whistleblower Directive) — transposition deadline
  17 December 2021 (17 December 2023 for 50–249 workers, art. 26). CZ transposed
  late: CJEU **C-152/23** *Commission v Czech Republic* (6 March 2025), lump sum
  EUR 2.3 m.
- **Zákon č. 171/2023 Sb., o ochraně oznamovatelů** (*ZOO*) — effective
  **1 August 2023** (§ 30). Employers with 50–249 employees (non-public) had to

  32/2025 Sb. (companion act to zákon č. 31/2025 Sb., o digitalizaci finančního
  trhu; adds MiCA crypto-asset entities to § 8(1)(e)); current consolidated version from 15 February 2025.
- **Zákon č. 172/2023 Sb.** (accompanying act) — reversed burden of proof
  (**§ 133a(2) OSŘ**); labour-inspection offences (**§ 34a zákona č. 251/2005 Sb.,
  o inspekci práce**); AML-act internal reporting duty (**§ 21(6)(b) zákona č.
  253/2008 Sb.**).

## 2. Scope tests

1. **Is the client a *povinný subjekt*?** (§ 8(1)) — any yes → internal system
   mandatory:
   - (a) public contracting authority (*veřejný zadavatel*), except municipality
     < 10,000 inhabitants and regional/municipal non-commercial bodies averaging
     < 50 employees (§ 8(1)(a), (4));
   - (b) employer with **≥ 50 employees as at 1 January** of the calendar year
     (headcount at that date, not an average);
   - (c) corporate-income-tax authorities; (d) civil aviation, maritime,
     offshore oil and gas entities;
   - (e) **regardless of headcount**: listed capital-market and investment-fund
     entities, CCPs and trade repositories, tied mortgage-credit agents, insurers,
     reinsurers, insurance intermediaries, IORPs, crypto-asset entities (MiCA);
   - **AML obliged persons** (*povinné osoby* under § 2 zákona č. 253/2008 Sb.,
     incl. advocates, auditors, tax advisors, accountants, real-estate agents) —
     regardless of size under § 21(6)(b) AML act, not under § 8 ZOO.
2. **Is the matter a reportable breach?** (§ 2(1)) — connected with work, and
   (a) features of a criminal offence; (b) administrative offence with fine
   ceiling **≥ CZK 100,000**; (c) breach of ZOO; (d) breach of law/EU law in the
   14 listed areas (financial services, CIT, AML, consumer protection, product
   safety, transport, environment, food/feed/animals, radiation/nuclear,
   competition and procurement, internal order and security, privacy and
   network security, EU financial interests, internal market).
3. **Is the reporter a protected person?** (§ 2(3)–(4)) — work in the broad
   sense: employees, applicants, self-employed, shareholders, statutory bodies,
   volunteers, trainees, suppliers' staff. Connected persons protected (§ 4(2)).
4. **Exclusions** (§ 3(3)): essential security interests, intelligence services,
   seal of confession. Reporting does not breach banking or contractual secrecy
   (§ 3(1)) but does not override classified information and listed
   professional secrecy (advocates, notaries, judges, prosecutors, executors,
   healthcare) (§ 3(2)).
5. **Protection conditions** (§ 7): report made internally, to MSp, or publicly
   under § 7(1)(c); reasonable grounds to believe the information was true; no
   protection for knowingly false reports (§ 7(3)).

## 3. Roles

- **Povinný subjekt** (§ 8) — must operate the internal reporting system.
- **Příslušná osoba** (designated person, §§ 10–11) — natural person, adult,
  full legal capacity, *bezúhonná* (§ 10(2)–(5)); receives and assesses reports,
  proposes measures, acts impartially, bound by confidentiality (lasts after
  the role ends); must not be penalised for duly performing the role (§ 11(2)).
- **Oznamovatel** (reporter, § 2(3)) — natural person reporting in connection
  with work; anonymous reporter protected once identity becomes known (§ 4(3)).
- **Osoba, proti níž oznámení směřuje** — person concerned; rights limited by
  identity confidentiality (§ 20).
- **Delegate operator** (§ 8(2)) — external provider/parent may run the system;
  responsibility stays with the povinný subjekt.
- **MSp** — external channel (§§ 13–18) and supervisor; **OIP/SÚIP** — supervisor
  for employers under § 6(1)(a) zákona o inspekci práce (§ 22(3)).

## 6. Enforcement and sanctions

- **Supervisors:** MSp (public bodies; § 26(1)(a), (f), (h)–(j)) and OIP/SÚIP for
  private employers subject to labour inspection (§ 22; offences in § 34a zákona
  o inspekci práce). MSp hears ZOO offences (§ 28) except where labour inspection
  is competent.
- **Fines (CZK, maxima):** obliged entity — failure to publish information
  (§ 26(1)(d)) or to comply with remedial order (j) **400,000**; allowing
  retaliation and other system failures (a)–(c), (e)–(i) **1,000,000** (§ 26(3)).
  Any person: retaliation **1,000,000** (§ 27(2), (4)); obstructing a report
  **100,000** (§ 27(1)); breach of confidentiality **80,000** (§ 27(3)).
  Reporter: knowingly false report **50,000** (§ 23). Příslušná osoba: up to
  20,000 / 50,000 / 100,000 (§ 24).
- **Civil:** compensation for non-pecuniary harm (§ 5); reversed burden of proof
  in retaliation disputes (§ 133a(2) OSŘ) — employer must show the measure was
  objectively justified and proportionate; unlawful dismissal under zákoník práce.
- **Practice:** enforcement light so far. MSp annual report 2024: 156 submissions
  to MSp (59 in scope), one CZK 5,000 fine for retaliation; 2025: 191 (41 in
  scope), one CZK 6,000 fine. Inspections target municipalities and state
  bodies; private-sector risk lies mainly in litigation (retaliation claims) and
  in M&A/compliance DD findings.

## 7. Contract touchpoints

- **Outsourced system / hotline agreement** (§ 8(2)): provider acts as or supports
  příslušná osoba; confidentiality; access limited to příslušná osoba; § 12
  deadlines as SLA; § 21 register and 5-year retention; art. 28 GDPR DPA
  (provider = processor); sub-processors and hosting location; exit/return of
  register.
- **Intra-group sharing** (§ 8(3)): only entities ≤ 249 employees; written
  arrangement; each entity names its příslušná osoba.
- **NDAs, settlement agreements, employment contracts, severance**: no clause may
  waive or restrict reporting (§ 6); carve-out for reports to authorities.
- **Supplier codes of conduct**: if non-worker reports are accepted (§ 9(2)(a)),
  point suppliers to the channel.
- **M&A**: warranties on whistleblowing system, open reports, retaliation claims;
  data-room disclosure must preserve reporter identity (§ 20).

## 8. Interaction with other regimes

- **GDPR (`gdpr`)** — legal basis art. 6(1)(c) (statutory duty §§ 9, 21) ⚠ verify
  ÚOOÚ position; art. 9/10 data possible; § 20 confidentiality restricts the
  person concerned's art. 14/15 rights (art. 23 GDPR restriction via ZOO);
  retention 5 years; DPIA screening for hotline tools.
- **AML (`aml-cz`)** — AML obliged persons: internal system under § 21(6)(b) AML
  act, anonymous reports required, outsourcing barred; reports may also trigger
  a suspicious-transaction notice to FAÚ (§ 18 AML act).
- **NIS2 / DORA / AI Act** — reports about cybersecurity or AI breaches fall in
  § 2(1)(d) areas (network security, financial services); AI Act art. 87 applies
  Directive 2019/1937 to AI Act breaches.
- **Internal investigations** — report triggers investigation plan: preserve
  identity confidentiality (§ 20) through interviews and the report; labour-law
  limits on employee monitoring (§ 316 zákoník práce); privilege — in-house
  counsel not covered by advocate secrecy (CJEU C-550/07 P *Akzo*); retaliation
  check before any measure against the reporter (§ 4); criminal-offence
  reporting duties (§ 368 trestní zákoník — non-reporting of listed crimes).
- **Professional secrecy** — advocates' secrecy is not overridden by ZOO (§ 3(2));
  advocates receiving reports as příslušná osoba must separate roles.
