# currency-watch — capability

**Contract.** The standing "is this still the law?" check: what to re-verify,
against which source, and how often. It owns *when to re-check*, never the
content — which regimes move is data and lives in the packs.

## The check

At the Gate Plan, and before any deliverable that relies on a rule whose status
can move:

1. **Pack freshness.** Read each loaded pack's `review_by`. Past it, the
   deliverable opens with the staleness line (`configure.md`) and every rule
   taken from that pack is checked against the current source, or reported as
   unchecked.
2. **Recent-developments test.** Ask whether the rule, its settled
   interpretation, or a pending amendment could have changed between the source's
   date and `EPOCH`. If yes, a current retrieval is mandatory (`ground.md`).
3. **Verify-at source and window.** For each moving proposition, name the source
   to re-check it against (the pack's §1 (Sources and hierarchy) portals) and how long a prior
   verification holds (`provenance.md`, verification log).

## Record

- Every moving rule carries its verify-at source and next-check date in the
  matter's registers (or the reviewer note when there is no folder).
- Known amendment dates (a pack's §1 (Sources and hierarchy)) are **scheduled**, not discovered late.
- The reviewer note's **Currency** line reports what was checked, when, and what
  is stale (`present.md`).

## Checks

- Every pack past its `review_by` produced the staleness line and a re-check, or
  an explicit "not checked".
- Every moving proposition names its verify-at source and window.
