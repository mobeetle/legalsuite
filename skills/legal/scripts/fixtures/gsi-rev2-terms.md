### Definitions

(a) "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls a Party.
(b) "Enterprise Customer" means a prospective or active third-party enterprise client.
(c) "Net Collected Revenue" means subscription fees actually received in cleared cash by JetBrains.
(d) "Wholesale Discount" means the discount percentage off the List Price.
(e) "Wholesale Discount Percentage" means the same discount stated as a rate.
(f) "Referral Commission" means the commission payable for a Registered Opportunity.
(g) "Referral Commission Percentage" means the commission rate stated as a percentage.

### Commercial Scale Pathways

(a) JetBrains shall pay Partner the Referral Commission for each Registered Opportunity.
(b) Partner may purchase subscriptions at the Wholesale Discount agreed in Annex B.

## Annex B: Commercial Scale Schedule

(a) The commission is calculated on Net Collected Revenue for the eligible period.
(b) The wholesale price equals the List Price minus the Wholesale Discount.
