MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

between Alpha Technologies (the "Discloser") and Beta Ventures (the "Recipient")

1. Definitions
1.1 "Confidential Information" means information disclosed in writing and clearly marked "confidential".
1.2 "Purpose" means evaluating a possible cooperation between the parties.

2. Obligations
2.1 The Recipient shall keep the Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the Purpose.
2.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, affiliates and advisers. The Recipient is not responsible for any disclosure by them.
2.3 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information if required by law or by any authority.

3. Exceptions
3.1 The obligations in clause 2 do not apply to information that is publicly available, already known to the Recipient, or independently developed.
3.2 Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Recipient's use of general knowledge, skills and experience, or of any information retained in the unaided memory of its personnel.

4. Feedback
4.1 Any feedback or suggestions provided by the Recipient may be used by both parties, and the Discloser grants the Recipient a perpetual licence to use any improvements developed by the Recipient based on the Confidential Information.

5. Term
5.1 This Agreement and the obligations under it end one (1) year after the date of the last disclosure.

6. Liability
6.1 The total liability of either party under this Agreement is limited to EUR 5,000.

7. Governing law
7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London have exclusive jurisdiction.

8. Final provisions
8.1 Amendments to this Agreement must be in writing. Clause 9.4 applies to notices.
