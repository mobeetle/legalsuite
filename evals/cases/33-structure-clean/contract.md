# Supply agreement

## 1. Definitions
"Goods" means the items listed in the annex.
"Supplier" means the party named in the preamble.

## 2. Delivery
2.1 The Supplier shall deliver the Goods by 1 May 2026.
2.2 Clause 2.1 applies from the effective date.

## 3. Payment
3.1 The Customer shall pay within 30 days of delivery.
3.2 Clause 2.2 applies to payment as well.
